# F4: 失败教训与反方观点

> 注：本文件由两轮子代理产出合并而成。[1]–[12] 为第一轮（SkillsBench/漂移/编排反例等）；[13]–[22] 为第二轮补充（Anthropic context-engineering、model collapse、长上下文边界等）。

## Findings

### [1] LLM 自撰写的技能在 SkillsBench 上收益为零，而人类撰写的技能提升通过率 16.2 个百分点——未经评估校准的"经验固化"直接失效
- quote: "On SkillsBench, human-authored skills improve pass rates by 16.2 percentage points, while LLM-authored skills provide no measurable gain."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10546
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-06-09
- confidence: medium

### [2] SoK（Agentic Skills 系统化综述）指出：精选（curated）技能可显著提升智能体成功率，而自生成技能可能反而拉低成功率
- quote: "anchored by recent benchmark evidence that curated skills can substantially improve agent success rates while self-generated skills may degrade them"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.20867
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-02-24
- confidence: medium

### [3] 用启发式反思或"原始成功计数"来更新已固化的技能/SOP 资产被实证判定为脆弱（轨迹稀疏、昂贵、上下文依赖时尤甚），Bayesian-Agent 的后验式修复将 RealFin-Bench 从 45% 提到 65%、SOP-Bench 从 80% 提到 95%
- quote: "current self-evolution pipelines often update these assets through heuristic reflection or raw success counts. Such updates are brittle when trajectories are sparse, expensive, and context-dependent."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08348
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-06-06
- confidence: medium

### [4] 固化记忆在"隐式冲突"（后续观察使旧记忆失效但无显式否定）下大面积失效：STALE 基准 400 个冲突场景上，前沿模型最佳总准确率仅 55.2%
- quote: "even the best evaluated model achieving only 55.2% overall accuracy"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06527
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-05-07
- confidence: medium

### [5] 环境分布漂移下固化经验的智能体表现崩塌：EvoArena（终端/软件/社交偏好三域渐进更新）上当前智能体平均准确率仅 39.6%
- quote: "current agents struggle on EvoArena, achieving an average accuracy of 39.6% across evolving terminal, software, and social-preference domains"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13681
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-06-11
- confidence: medium

### [6] MemGuard 识别出经验库的两个结构性失败模式：不可靠准入（失败轨迹/偶发成功混入并误导后续决策）与记忆漂移（长期积累的重复、过期、冲突记录仅靠检索无法修复）——即固化资产的持续维护成本
- quote: "The second is memory drift: long-running banks accumulate duplicate, stale, and conflicting records that retrieval alone cannot repair."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21867
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-08-22
- confidence: medium

### [7] 固化计划（固化为可复用程序/计划的部分）在需求变更后 100% 出错：30 个五智能体工作流中插入规划后修订，仅做新鲜度检查的执行器每次都执行过期计划（0/30→30/30 需显式追踪推导输入版本）
- quote: "In 30 live five-agent workflows with a revision inserted after planning, a freshness-only executor acts on the stale plan every time"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03340
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-09-03
- confidence: medium

### [8] "模型迭代使固化资产贬值"的直接实证：PromptBridge 将"为模型 A 调好的 prompt 复用到模型 B 后显著变差"命名为 Model Drifting，并称其"普遍且严重"——固化提示词资产与模型版本强绑定
- quote: "Yet prompts are highly model-sensitive: reusing a prompt engineered for one model on another often yields substantially worse performance than a prompt optimized for the target model. We term this phenomenon Model Drifting. Through extensive empirical analysis across diverse LLM configurations, we show that model drifting is both common and severe."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01420
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-12-01
- confidence: medium

### [9] 固化外部编排（LangGraph 式路由/状态机）被对照实验判负：同模型下编排系统在旅行预订/Zoom 支持/保险理赔上失败 24%/9%/17%，而把整个流程放进 system prompt 让模型自编排仅 11.5%/0.5%/5%——且"外部编排只是早期模型的需要"
- quote: "While external orchestration may have been necessary for earlier models, advances in frontier model capabilities have made it unnecessary for multi-turn conversations following a defined procedure."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27891
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-04-30
- confidence: medium

### [10] Anthropic 官方给出"何时保留 LLM/何时固化为 workflow"的边界：定义良好的任务用 workflow（可预期、可重复），需要灵活性与模型驱动决策的规模化场景才用 agent；多数应用单次 LLM 调用+检索+示例即够
- quote: "workflows offer predictability and consistency for well-defined tasks, whereas agents are the better option when flexibility and model-driven decision-making are needed at scale. For many applications, however, optimizing single LLM calls with retrieval and in-context examples is usually enough."
- url: https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-12-19
- confidence: high

### [11] "distillation/路由 vs 持续调用大模型"争论的经济学一极：RouteLLM（LMSYS 开源）用 Chatbot Arena 数据训练路由器，相对只用 GPT-4 在 MT Bench/MMLU/GSM8K 上分别降本 85%+/45%/35%，同时保住 95% 的 GPT-4 性能——即"不必每步都调最大模型"
- quote: "cost reductions of over 85% on MT Bench, 45% on MMLU, and 35% on GSM8K as compared to using only GPT-4, while still achieving 95% of GPT-4's performance"
- url: https://lmsys.org/blog/2024-07-01-routellm/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-07-01
- confidence: high

### [12] 争论的反方一极：Position 论文反对"把企业任务交给 LLM 或蒸馏成小模型"，主张 LLM 只作接口、确定性计算与知识外包给符号程序——同时构成"确定性任务→固化代码、而非蒸馏"的边界主张
- quote: "While these are often addressed through large language model (LLM) deployment or distillation into smaller models, we argue this is inefficient, unreliable, and misaligned with enterprise task structures. Instead, AI systems should treat language models as interfaces rather than monolithic engines"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09365
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-05-10
- confidence: medium

### [13] Anthropic 将"在 prompt/规则中硬编码复杂 if-else 逻辑"列为典型失败模式：制造脆弱性并随时间推高维护成本
- quote: "At one extreme, we see engineers hardcoding complex, brittle logic in their prompts to elicit exact agentic behavior. This approach creates fragility and increases maintenance complexity over time."
- url: https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-09-29
- confidence: high

### [14] Anthropic 反对"把每条边界 case 堆进规则清单"的固化方式，主张用少量 canonical 示例替代规则穷举
- quote: "teams will often stuff a laundry list of edge cases into a prompt in an attempt to articulate every possible rule the LLM should follow for a particular task. We do not recommend this."
- url: https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-09-29
- confidence: high

### [15] 模型迭代直接贬值"指令型固化资产"：Anthropic 明言更强模型需要更少的规定性工程，prompt 格式细节正变得不重要
- quote: "We're already seeing that smarter models require less prescriptive engineering, allowing agents to operate with more autonomy."
- url: https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-09-29
- confidence: high

### [16] "长上下文取代检索/缓存"并非无条件成立：Anthropic 引用 context rot 研究指出上下文是有限注意力预算，所有模型随 token 增加回忆能力下降
- quote: "as the number of tokens in the context window increases, the model's ability to accurately recall information from that context decreases... While some models exhibit more gentle degradation than others, this characteristic emerges across all models."
- url: https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-09-29
- confidence: high

### [17] 2024-12 评测论文给出长上下文 vs RAG 的边界条件：长上下文在 QA 基准总体占优，但对话类与通用查询仍由 RAG 占优，块级检索明显落后
- quote: "We show that LC generally outperforms RAG in question-answering benchmarks, especially for Wikipedia-based questions. Summarization-based retrieval performs comparably to LC, while chunk-based retrieval lags behind. However, RAG has advantages in dialogue-based and general question queries."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.01880
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-12-27
- confidence: high

### [18] Anthropic 对"何时保留 LLM 而非固化为固定路径"给出明确边界：步骤数不可预测、无法硬编码固定路径的开放问题才用 agent
- quote: "Agents can be used for open-ended problems where it's difficult or impossible to predict the required number of steps, and where you can't hardcode a fixed path... For many applications, however, optimizing single LLM calls with retrieval and in-context examples is usually enough."
- url: https://www.anthropic.com/research/building-effective-agents
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-12-19
- confidence: high

### [19] 持续调用大模型一侧的成本/风险被官方量化：agentic 系统以延迟和成本换任务表现，自主性带来更高成本与错误复利——但 Anthropic 同时要求复杂度必须"可证明地改善结果"才引入
- quote: "Agentic systems often trade latency and cost for better task performance... The autonomous nature of agents means higher costs, and the potential for compounding errors... you should consider adding complexity *only* when it demonstrably improves outcomes."
- url: https://www.anthropic.com/research/building-effective-agents
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-12-19
- confidence: high

### [20] Nature 论文证明"用模型生成物递归固化/再训练"会导致不可逆退化（model collapse）：分布长尾先消失，方差坍缩，且 fine-tuning 无法幸免——直接对应"蒸馏/固化进参数或程序后泛化变差"的风险
- quote: "We find that indiscriminate use of model-generated content in training causes irreversible defects in the resulting models, in which tails of the original content distribution disappear."
- url: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07566-y
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-07-24
- confidence: high

### [21] 实践者（HN，2026-08）报告模型更新会原生内化新的工程技法，使此前积累的 harness/图编排类固化技能贬值
- quote: "Whenever I try to learn and apply a new AI engineering technique, the next model update already does it out of the box, making the effort feel pointless."
- url: https://hn.algolia.com/api/v1/items/49481969
- source_type: community
- published: 2026-08-28
- confidence: medium

### [22] 预先固化/预索引的检索资产会过期：Anthropic 称 Claude Code 用 just-in-time 的 glob/grep 检索"绕过 stale indexing 问题"，并预测模型能力提升后人工固化与策展会持续减少
- quote: "primitives like glob and grep allow it to navigate its environment and retrieve files just-in-time, effectively bypassing the issues of stale indexing... As model capabilities improve, agentic design will trend towards letting intelligent models act intelligently, with progressively less human curation."
- url: https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-09-29
- confidence: high

# F5: 量化效果与经济学

## Findings

### [1] Compile-and-execute（一次性 LLM 编译 + 本地程序执行）把 5 步工作流 × 500 次迭代的推理成本从约 $150（持续 LLM 循环）降到约 $15（即使激进缓存）再到 <$0.10（固化执行）
- quote: "For a 5-step workflow over 500 iterations, a continuous agent incurs approximately 150.00 USD in inference costs; even with aggressive caching, this remains near 15.00 USD. We propose a Compile-and-Execute architecture that decouples LLM reasoning from browser execution, reducing per-workflow inference cost to under 0.10 USD."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09718
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-04-08 (v1), 2026-04-25 (v2)
- confidence: medium

### [2] 固化执行把推理复杂度从 O(M×N)（迭代数×步数）摊销为 O(1)：LLM 只编译一次产出确定性 JSON 工作流蓝图，本地运行时零额外模型调用驱动执行
- quote: "A one-shot LLM invocation processes a token-efficient semantic representation from a DOM Sanitization Module (DSM) and emits a deterministic JSON workflow blueprint. A lightweight runtime then drives the browser without further model queries. We formalize this cost reduction from O(M x N) to amortized O(1) inference scaling"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09718
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-04-08 (v1), 2026-04-25 (v2)
- confidence: medium

### [3] 编译出的固化工作流零样本成功率 80–94%，少量 HITL 修补后执行可靠性接近 100%，单次编译成本仅 $0.002–$0.092（5 个前沿模型）
- quote: "zero-shot compilation success rates of 80-94%. Crucially, the modularity of the JSON intermediate representation allows minimal Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) patching to elevate execution reliability to near-100%. At per-compilation costs between 0.002 USD and 0.092 USD across five frontier models"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09718
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-04-08 (v1), 2026-04-25 (v2)
- confidence: medium

### [4] DeepSeek 官方定价中缓存命中输入比未命中便宜 30–50 倍（deepseek-flash off-peak 每 1M token $0.003 vs $0.15；deepseek-v4-pro $0.022 vs $0.66）
- quote: "1M INPUT TOKENS (CACHE HIT) OFF-PEAK $0.003 $0.022 … 1M INPUT TOKENS (CACHE MISS) OFF-PEAK $0.15 $0.66"
- url: https://api-docs.deepseek.com/quick_start/pricing
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (fetched 2026-10-15)
- confidence: high

### [5] KV 缓存复用（CacheBlend，3 个开源模型 × 4 个基准数据集）把 TTFT 降低 2.2–3.3 倍、吞吐提升 2.8–5 倍且不损失生成质量
- quote: "CacheBlend reduces time-to-first-token (TTFT) by 2.2-3.3x and increases the inference throughput by 2.8-5x from full KV recompute without compromising generation quality."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.16444
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-05-26 (v1), 2025-04-03 (v3)
- confidence: high

### [6] 语义缓存命中的响应延迟约为直接调用 LLM 的 1/10（ContextCache，真实多轮对话评测）
- quote: "cached responses exhibit approximately 10 times lower latency than direct LLM invocation, enabling significant computational cost reductions for LLM conversational applications."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.22791
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-06-28 (v1), 2025-07-15 (v3)
- confidence: medium

### [7] 朴素缓存复用的质量风险可量化：HotpotQA 上 15–35%、文档漂移场景 51.5% 的查询会收到错误缓存答案；证据校验门禁可压到 0.0%/1.5%，代价是 p50 延迟仅为无缓存基线的 1.04–1.07 倍
- quote: "GroundedCache drives USR to 0.0% on every HotpotQA regime (vs. 15-35% under naive caching) and to 1.5% on mtRAG document drift (vs. 51.5%) ... while end-to-end p50 latency stays within 1.04-1.07x of a no-cache RAG baseline."
- url: https://arxiv.org/search/?query=semantic+caching+LLM+cost+latency+reduction&searchtype=all
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-05-26
- confidence: medium

### [8] NVIDIA ASPIRE：把验证过的经验固化为可复用控制程序库后，零样本新任务成功率 31%，而依赖测试时推理+重试的先前方法仅 4%（LIBERO-Pro Long）
- quote: "on LIBERO-Pro Long, ASPIRE achieves 31% success versus 4% for prior methods despite their use of test-time reasoning and retries."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00272
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-06-30
- confidence: high

### [9] RESOURCE2SKILL：将经验蒸馏为可执行技能（含代码）的软件智能体，在 7 个创作域上比无技能智能体平均提分 +11.9 个百分点，并在 28 个模型-域格子中赢得 26 个
- quote: "Across seven practical authoring domains, RESOURCE2SKILL improves average overall score by +11.9 percentage points over no-skill agents and outperforms strong harness baselines in 26 of 28 main-aggregate model-domain cells."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29538
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-06-28 (v1), 2026-07-17 (v4)
- confidence: medium

### [10] 生产环境实测：语义缓存 + fetch-once/process-locally 等 6 种 token 优化模式使多智能体工作流 token 消耗估计下降 60–70%，冷加载延迟从约 3.5–10.5 分钟降到 61–116 秒（6 次计时）
- quote: "In production they cut measured cold-load latency to 61-116 seconds (six timed runs) from an operational baseline of roughly 3.5-10.5 minutes, with an estimated 60-70% token reduction."
- url: https://arxiv.org/search/?query=semantic+caching+LLM+cost+latency+reduction&searchtype=all
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-08-17
- confidence: medium

### [11] 多智能体管线中引入语义缓存使 LLM 调用次数减少 41.6%，并同步降低延迟与能耗（301 个注入类提示的受控实验）
- quote: "semantic caching delivers substantial computational savings, achieving a 41.6% reduction in LLM calls and corresponding decreases in latency, energy consumption, and carbon emissions."
- url: https://arxiv.org/search/?query=semantic+caching+LLM+cost+latency+reduction&searchtype=all
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-01-19
- confidence: medium
